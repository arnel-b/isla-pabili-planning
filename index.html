Current concept

“Bili mo ako nito.”

You write the list. A rider buys it. It arrives at your door.

  1. Customer writes a pabili list

    Anything they need, not tied to one store

  2. A rider accepts it

    Available riders see the request

  3. The rider buys the items

    And keeps the customer updated

  4. Delivered to the door

    The customer confirms receipt

Pabili comes first. Merchant catalogs can come later.

Proposal

The first beta.

Just the basics, done reliably.

In the first beta

  • Custom pabili list, with photo
  • Rider accepts and updates the order
  • Receipt photo shown to the customer
  • Call or message the rider
  • Handling unavailable items, cancellations and refused orders
  • Simple fare To decide
  • COD plus manual GCash prepay Rule to decide

Later, not now

Live GPS tracking Merchant catalogs In-app GCash or Maya payments Ratings and reviews Promo vouchers
